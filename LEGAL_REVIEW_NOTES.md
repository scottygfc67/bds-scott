# Legal owner review notes

The public Privacy Policy and Website Terms are limited to facts verified in the repository and current hosting configuration. Before treating them as final legal documents, the site owner should confirm:

1. The full legal identity of the Briggs Digital Solutions data controller and website operator, including trading status and a correspondence address. The repository establishes only the name, Belfast location and contact email.
2. Whether the Vercel account is covered by the current Vercel Data Processing Addendum and UK international-transfer terms, and which production processing regions/account log-retention settings apply.
3. The physical hosting location and retention/logging configuration of the Briggs-controlled Postal installation.
4. The provider that hosts the `info@briggsdigitalsolutions.com` mailbox and that provider’s processing locations, retention and contractual safeguards.
5. The actual operational retention schedule for unsuccessful enquiries, successful enquiries, proposals, client correspondence and technical logs. The policy currently uses criteria rather than an invented fixed period.
6. Whether enquiry details are manually copied into any CRM, project-management, accounting, cloud-storage or other system outside this repository. Any such recipient or processor should be added to the Privacy Policy.
7. Whether any platform-level Vercel feature not represented in the codebase sets cookies or enables analytics, speed insights, firewall/bot tooling or session replay in production. The application code itself includes none of these.
8. Whether Briggs needs to identify an ICO registration/fee status or a dedicated postal route for data-protection complaints in its internal compliance records.

The copy should receive professional legal review, particularly after these operational facts are confirmed.
